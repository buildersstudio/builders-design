      A read, not a random number.

      Every hook is read on three things people have responded to for a century of behavioural research. The science is how it works; the answer is in plain words.

      Relatability

      Whether the viewer recognises a moment, a problem or a person like them in the first line.

      Tension

      Whether the line opens a gap (a question, a contradiction, a mistake) that only the rest of the post closes.

      Reward

      Whether it is clear, and specific, what the viewer gets for staying: a result, a number, a method.
