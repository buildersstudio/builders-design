Agentic AI that analyzes content performance through behavioral and contextual signals. Learns what resonates.

Evidence so far: Currently validating across multiple content verticals.

## Product

Trigger is an AI tool for organic social content that describes itself as the cognitive layer of content, reading posts through behavioral science rather than counting likes. Through a chat interface, users can dissect a post, generate hooks and compare their outlier posts against a cohort of peer accounts. It reads what is actually in the image, not only the engagement numbers, and places the user's account at the center with the profiles they compete against around it. Users connect their Instagram account, and a free tier includes saved chats, unlimited reference profiles and three post analyses a month.

## Customer

Organic content creators, social media managers and content strategists who want to understand why their posts perform and how they compare with their peers.
