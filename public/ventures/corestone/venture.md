## Product

Corestone turns the data that comes back from ground investigation into validated tables and report drawings. It takes drillhole data and drill logs, checks them, and produces the structured output that engineers need for their reports. An AI layer reads borehole wall imagery to support fracture and rock quality analysis. Drill logs are the first case, and the same approach extends to other field documents in construction, mining and energy.

## Customer

Geotechnical and civil engineering consultancies and drilling contractors that handle large volumes of ground investigation data.
