Learn a language by living a story: illustrated story chapters you speak your way through out loud with a real-time voice tutor, while every word and grammar point you did not know becomes a flashcard.

## Product

Katasaga is a web and iOS app for learners who already have a basic foundation (A2 or above is recommended) and want to move from knowing the words to holding a conversation. You pick a story, such as The Barcelona Contact (spy, mystery, travel), Where the Map Ends (adventure, mystery) or Love, Between Departures (romance, travel), and play it chapter by chapter: each chapter opens with a briefing and goals, and you pass it by reading documents, paying attention to details and speaking in real time with the characters, who answer in unscripted voice with native accents. After each chapter you get an analysis of your performance, and the vocabulary and grammar you struggled with are captured automatically as explanation cards for spaced repetition. Outside the stories there are scenarios (cafés, travel, interviews), freeform conversation and oral exam practice for GCSE, A-level and IB, where you read and analyse resources and then discuss them out loud before getting a detailed review. It supports Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and English, voice audio is never stored, and Premium is planned at $19.99 a month or $199 a year with a 7-day free trial, next to a free plan with the first chapters of a story; the beta opens to a first cohort of 200 users at the start of the academic year, with the public launch at the start of 2027.

## Customer

Learners aged 16 and over who have done the apps, know the words and the rules and still cannot hold a real conversation, including students preparing for GCSE, A-level and IB oral exams in Spanish, French or German.
