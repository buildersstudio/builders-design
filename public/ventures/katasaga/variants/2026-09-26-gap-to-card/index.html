Memorization only gets you so far.

You have done the apps. You know the words and the rules, but you still cannot hold a real conversation.

Katasaga is the next step for learners with an A2 foundation or above: you speak out loud inside a story, and every knowledge gap you did not know you had is captured in the background.

Inside the conversation

The card appears the moment you need it.

You try to order and do not know how to say cheese omelette. Katasaga explains it on the spot, then keeps it for your spaced repetition.

  • Vocabulary
    Una tortilla de queso
    Cheese omelette
  • When to use
    Ordering an omelette made with cheese
  • Example
    También me gustaría una tortilla de queso, por favor.
After every chapter

A review of what you said, and what you could not.

Each chapter ends with an analysis of your performance. Vocabulary and grammar you struggled with are listed, explained and added to your flashcards.

Your weakest areas, in one deck.

  • Auto-captured from live conversations
  • Grammar patterns, not just vocabulary
  • Adaptable spaced repetition
  • Detailed analytics and level estimates
Speaking practice

The same capture, wherever you speak.

A real-time voice tutor with native accents holds the conversation, in scenarios built for your level or for the exam you have coming up.

Scenarios

Cafés, travel, interviews and more, tailored to your level. You can add your own templates.

Freeform

Speak naturally about any topic when you just want a casual chat.

Oral exams

GCSE, A-level and IB preparation: read and analyse the resources, use them out loud, then get a detailed review.

Where the gaps come from

Stories that make you say things you would not drill.

Every story unfolds in chapters, each with its own setting, characters and a moment where you have to speak your way through. The same stories are playable in every supported language.
