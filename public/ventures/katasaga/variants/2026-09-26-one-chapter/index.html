Part 2 · The conversation

You have done the drills. Now have the conversation.

A real-time voice tutor plays every character in the scene. You answer out loud, the story reacts, and the chapter only moves on when you have done what the briefing asked.

  • Unscripted voiceReal-time replies with native accents, not a fixed dialogue tree.
  • Built for your levelScenarios tailored to where you are. An A2 foundation is the recommended start.
  • Private by defaultVoice audio is used live and never stored. Only the transcript is kept, so you can pick up where you left off.
Part 3 · The review

What you did not know gets captured automatically.

After each chapter you get an analysis of your performance. The words and grammar you struggled with become explanation cards for spaced repetition, so the next chapter starts from your weakest areas.

Grammar patterns, not just vocabulary.

Cards are made from the gaps in your own conversations, with an example sentence and when to use it.

  • Auto-captured from live conversations
  • Adaptable spaced repetition
  • Detailed analytics and level estimates

For students

The same chapter format as your oral exam.

Dedicated exam-prep sessions for GCSE, A-level and IB work the way most oral exams do.

  1. You are given resources to read and analyse.
  2. You use them out loud in the examination practice.
  3. You get a detailed review of exactly what to improve.
