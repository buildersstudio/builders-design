What the story leaves you with

Every chapter ends with a deck of your own gaps.

After each chapter you get an analysis of your performance. Words and grammar you did not know are captured automatically and kept, story by story, for spaced repetition.

  • Explanation cards with when to use it and an example
  • Grammar patterns, not just vocabulary
  • Voice audio is never stored, only the transcript
For students

Approach your oral exam with confidence.

Dedicated exam-prep sessions for GCSE, A-level and IB: you are given resources to analyse and read, then use them in the examination practice, the same format used in most oral exams, followed by a detailed review.
