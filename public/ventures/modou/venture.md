Which construction project to call today, why it is live now, and who decides.

## Product

Modou is a project intelligence layer for companies that sell into construction. It reads the public documents where projects surface and move, such as official announcements, tenders, permits and grid-operator publications, every working day. From those signals it tells a supplier which project to pursue, why it matters now and who the current decision-maker is, before the specification closes. It also checks whether a project is real, funded and still in a phase where a product can be chosen, and favours a few strong opportunities over a long list of weak ones.

## Customer

Commercial teams at building-product manufacturers and suppliers in the Netherlands, who need to reach the right projects at the right moment.
