Evidence-based venture intelligence. Everything a portfolio of ventures already produces, turned into positions you can check: where each venture stands, and the evidence behind it.

## Product

Krans reads what ventures already produce, from customer calls and internal meetings to Slack threads, documents and pipeline numbers, and keeps a living model of each venture that every new signal updates. Calls arrive from Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Granola, Read.ai or a transcript upload, and documents from Notion and Google Drive, and every learning is cited to who said it and when and graded on an evidence scale from E0 (vague) to E5 (they put something at stake). The same thing heard across several calls hardens into a signal, and evidence ages, so a claim nobody has repeated for sixty days loses weight along with the positions built on it. On top of that model sit Threads, which join every conversation with a company into one story, Positions, which turn the evidence into scored decisions with options, venture pages with stage, warmth and close probability, a portfolio overview sorted by which venture needs attention, and Reflections that review each call minutes after it ends. Ask Krans answers questions from the venture's own sources, and an MCP server brings the same context into Claude or Cursor.

## Customer

Portfolio holders that run more ventures in parallel than anyone can sit in calls for: venture studios, corporate venture builders, and funds and platform teams. Krans is built on learnings from a decade of ventures built inside Builders.
