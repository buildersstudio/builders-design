The problem

Loud is not the same as true.

A company hears more than anyone can read: customer calls, internal meetings, Slack threads, documents, pipeline numbers. So decisions get taken from the last call, or from whoever says it most often. Krans keeps the record of what was actually said, and how much each piece of it can carry.

How it grades

Each learning is cut down to what someone actually said.

Every call and document becomes its smallest defensible units: a passage in someone’s own words, pinned to who said it and when, and graded for how much it can carry. Nobody writes a summary, and nobody types anything in.
