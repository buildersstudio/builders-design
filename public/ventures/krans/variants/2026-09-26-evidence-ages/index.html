Grades

The grade sets how much a learning can carry.

Every learning is cited to who said it and when, and graded from E0 to E5. On this page, as in the model, a heavier grade is set in a heavier weight.

E0
Inferred
No direct quote
E1
Vague
A general or soft quote
E2
Specific
A clear statement, with context
E3
Impact
Quantified or described impact
E4
Behavioural
Spend, a workaround, a process change
E5
Committed
A pilot, an intro, a payment, their data
