01Inputs

02Method

How a sentence becomes evidence.

Each input is broken into its smallest defensible unit, a passage in someone’s own words, and placed against the part of the model it touches. Four rules decide how much it carries.

2.1

Cited

Every learning is pinned to who said it and when, and links back to the call, thread or document it came from.

2.2

Graded

Every learning sits on an evidence scale, from E0, vague, to E5, they put something at stake.

2.3

Hardened

One buyer saying it is noise. The same thing heard across several calls hardens into a signal.

2.4

Aged

A claim nobody has repeated for sixty days loses weight, along with the positions built on it.

03The evidence scale

Not every sentence weighs the same.

A passing remark and a signed budget are both evidence, and they are not worth the same. Krans grades every learning before it touches the model.

E0

Vague. A feeling, a rumour, a remark without a source.

E3

A concrete quote with impact.

E5

They put something at stake.

04Ageing

Evidence ages. So does confidence.

Nothing stays validated on the strength of one conversation from last spring. What you see is the state of the evidence today, not the day it was first heard.

  • Decay is automaticand visible on every claim and position.
  • Promotion never runs backwardsA signal that reached decision-grade keeps its history. Its weight is what moves.
  • Fresh evidence restores itin a day, the next time someone says it.
05Decisions

From evidence to the decisions that are yours to make.

Krans reads everything it knows about your company and pulls out the decisions in front of you. Each one says what it noticed, which way it would go and why, and what the other options would need to be true.

Needs you, or needs data

Krans will not take a view it cannot support.

A position marked Needs you is ready to decide. One marked Needs data says so, and waits for the evidence instead of guessing.

It argues against itself

Ask what would have to be true for the option it turned down.

It answers with your own evidence, so the case against a decision is made before the decision, not after it.
