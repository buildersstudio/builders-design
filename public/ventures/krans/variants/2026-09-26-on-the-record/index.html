02Weighed

Krans grades every learning on an evidence scale, from E0, something vague, to E5, where they put something at stake. A passing remark and a commitment never carry the same weight in your model.

03Hardened

Heard once, it is a learning. Heard across calls, it hardens into a signal.

One buyer saying it is noise. When the same thing keeps coming up across calls and threads, Krans joins it into one signal and tracks it week by week, and it fades again when nobody repeats it.

04Aged

Today, it carries weight.

Sixty days later, nobody has said it again.

Evidence ages. A claim nobody has repeated for sixty days loses weight, and so do the positions built on it, so your model never rests on something only one call believed.
