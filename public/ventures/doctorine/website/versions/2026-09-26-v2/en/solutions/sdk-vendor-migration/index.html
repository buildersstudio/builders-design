Migrating off a shut-down SDK vendor, with proof of what changed

When Anthropic acquired Stainless, every hosted product was wound down on 18 May 2026, and teams lost the pipeline behind their SDKs. Doctorine imports supported configuration, regenerates the surfaces and gates them, so you can show your users what did and did not change.

The problem

What a forced move puts at risk

  • The generator behind your published SDKs is gone, but your users still depend on its type names.
  • The next vendor could be acquired too.
  • Nobody can show, with evidence, that the regenerated SDK behaves the same.

Source: Anthropic, Anthropic acquires Stainless, 18 May 2026. www.anthropic.com

FAQ

Questions

Which configurations can you import?

Supported configuration from Fern, Stainless, Speakeasy or ReadMe becomes a Doctorine draft. The mapping record lists what mapped exactly, what degraded and what needs a decision.

How do we know nothing broke for our users?

Your existing tasks run against the regenerated SDK in the gate before anything is published, and the receipt records what shipped.
