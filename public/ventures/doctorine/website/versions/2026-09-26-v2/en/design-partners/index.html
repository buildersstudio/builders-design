What should we send for a free eval report?

Your public docs URL, a link to your OpenAPI spec if it is public, the two or three tasks your integrators do first and your role. No credentials, no private production data.

Three to six months, chosen before you start. You pay monthly and use Doctorine on a real OpenAPI spec every week.

There are 20 seats for individuals and 20 for companies. When they fill, we stop taking new partners. The free eval report stays open.
