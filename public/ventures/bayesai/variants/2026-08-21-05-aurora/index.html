How it works

Two failure modes. We catch both.

Every tool is totalled against your database truth, and judged against its own arithmetic. Nothing else on the market does either.

Discrepancy

Two tools record the same phenomenon and disagree. We total every source against your database and flag the gap — with the expected range your consent rate allows.

Anomaly

One tool contradicts its own arithmetic or history — no second source needed. Opens can't exceed deliveries; purchases can't exceed checkouts.

Every metric lives on a curve. An email open rate of 127% doesn't sit in the unusual tail — it sits past the edge of arithmetic. Flagged instantly, no second source needed.
