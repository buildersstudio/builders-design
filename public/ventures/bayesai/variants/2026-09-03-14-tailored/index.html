How it works

Three failure modes.
We catch all of them.

Every tool is totalled against your database truth, judged against its own arithmetic, and scanned for data that should never be there. Nothing else on the market does all three.

Discrepancies

Two tools record the same phenomenon and disagree. We total every source against your database and flag the gap — with the expected range your consent rate allows.
