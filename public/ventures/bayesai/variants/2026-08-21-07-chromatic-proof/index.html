01 The premise

Your dashboards show what happened. They cannot prove it happened correctly.

Marketing runs on numbers reported by tools with different incentives, definitions and failure modes. BayesAI gives them one standard: reality.

Every source is reconciled to the transactional backend. Every metric is tested against its own logic. Every gap is traced to a cause your team can act on. Quiet when the numbers hold. Exact when they do not.
