Marketing data you can trust, checked against your own database truth.

## Product

BayesAI is a continuous verification platform for marketing data. It audits the tools a company reports from, such as GA4, Meta, TikTok, Adobe, the CRM and the consent platform, against the company's own backend database and against plain arithmetic. It flags discrepancies, mathematically impossible numbers, PII leaks and broken dataLayers, and explains the root cause in plain English. Because it treats analytics, ads, ERP and the data layer as one whole, the same discrepancy shows up in one place, and anomalies can be traced back to the release that caused them.

## Customer

Marketing and data leaders at brands and agencies that spend heavily on advertising and report their numbers to a board, and need to trust every figure before they scale spend.
