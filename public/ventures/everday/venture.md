AI-powered skill mapping that connects workforce data across your entire organization in real time.

Evidence so far: Willingness-to-pay validated at enterprise tier.

## Product

Everday provides AI agents that run daily inside a company's existing HR tools to support hiring, internal mobility and skills decisions. The agents screen and rank candidates into shortlists with evidence, propose internal matches for open roles, keep employee skills passports current, and keep salary, scarcity and demand data fresh on every open role. Every fact carries a source and every recommendation carries its reasoning, and the site states that there is no automated decision-making, so people make the final call. It connects to tools including Greenhouse, Recruitee, Personio, BambooHR, HiBob, Notion and Slack. Everday is offered either as a managed service that the team builds and runs for the client, or as self-serve early access.

## Customer

HR and people operations teams at organizations that need explainable, defensible decisions on hiring, mobility and skills, with the EU AI Act in view. The site cites its work with ECHT Onderwijs on moving candidates into teaching roles.
