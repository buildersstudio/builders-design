AI agents that replace spreadsheet-driven FP&A. Real-time financial planning for fast-moving SMBs.

Evidence so far: Pain point confirmed in 3 independent segments.

## Product

Cortena is an AI operator for accounts payable that takes each invoice from receipt to a booking-ready state. It captures invoices from email, drives and e-invoices, including the UBL, CII, ZUGFeRD and Factur-X formats, and codes each line to account, cost center and VAT against the company's own chart of accounts and rules. Invoices then move through approval workflows that the team can rearrange in a workflow builder, with people kept in the loop. Booking-ready data is exported to accounting software such as DATEV, Exact and Twinfield, and the platform connects to more than 2,000 banks via Yapily. It handles multiple entities from one inbox and keeps a GoBD-aligned audit trail of every step.

## Customer

Finance teams in SMEs that want to do more without adding headcount, and accounting firms that want to serve more clients per accountant. The site names VC and family offices, high tech companies and food and beverage businesses among the sectors it serves.
