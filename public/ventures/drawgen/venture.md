From 3D model to dimensioned manufacturing drawing, in one click inside SOLIDWORKS.

## Product

drawgen is a SOLIDWORKS add-in that reads a 3D assembly and returns a finished, dimensioned drawing for every part in it, as a PDF and a DXF. Every dimension is measured off the solid model, so nothing is estimated or typed in by hand, and anything that needs engineering judgement is flagged rather than filled in. The add-in runs in-process, so the geometry never leaves the engineer's machine, and the same model produces the same sheet on every run. Parts lists, cut lists with material, and quotations derived from the same assembly are in development.

## Customer

Engineers at engineer-to-order manufacturers and machine builders who design in SOLIDWORKS and still redraw every part by hand after the 3D model is finished.
