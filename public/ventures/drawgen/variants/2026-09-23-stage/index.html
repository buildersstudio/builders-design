Measured, not estimated01

Every dimension measured off the solid. Nothing estimated, nothing invented.

drawgen takes every value on the sheet directly from the geometry of the model, so there is nothing typed in by hand. Anything unclear is flagged for you rather than filled in, because the calls that need engineering judgement stay with the engineer.

How it works02

Six steps between the finished model and the finished sheet.

01

Resolve

Read the open assembly and resolve every unique part inside it.

02

Classify

Work out what kind of part each one is, so it is drawn the way that kind of part should be.

03

Project

Project the views from the solid and place them on the sheet.

04

Dimension

Measure every dimension off the solid model. Nothing is estimated or typed in by hand.

05

Annotate

Complete the sheet with its notes and title block.

06

Gate

Check the sheet before it is released. Anything unclear is flagged for you rather than filled in.
