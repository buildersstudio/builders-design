Six steps between the finished model and the finished sheet.

The assembly goes in once. Each unique part comes out as its own dimensioned drawing, and the last step checks the result before anything is written.

04Principle

Measured, not estimated.

Every dimension is measured off the solid. Nothing estimated, nothing invented. Anything unclear is flagged for you rather than filled in, so the sheet you check is the sheet you can trust.
