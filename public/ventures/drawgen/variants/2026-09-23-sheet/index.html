BB
Pipeline

Six steps between the finished model and the finished sheet.

The assembly goes in once. Each unique part comes out as its own dimensioned drawing, and the last step checks the result before anything is written.

Resolve

01

One assembly, every part. Each unique part in the open assembly is found and separated.

Classify

02

Each part is read for what it is, from the model itself.

Project

03

The part becomes its own drawing, with the views it needs.

Dimension

04

Dimensions measured from the model, off the solid. None typed in.

Annotate

05

Callouts and notes applied the same way on every sheet.

Gate

06

Flagged, never filled in. Anything unclear is left for the engineer.

CC
Principle

Measured, not estimated.

Every dimension is measured off the solid. Nothing estimated, nothing invented. Anything unclear is flagged for you rather than filled in, so the sheet you check is the sheet you can trust.

DD
On your machine

Your geometry stays where it is. Your sheets stay the same.

Built for engineers at engineer-to-order manufacturers and machine builders who design in SOLIDWORKS, and who should not have to redraw a part once its 3D model is finished.

Geometry never leaves the engineer's machine.

drawgen runs in-process inside SOLIDWORKS. It reads the model where it already lives, and the PDF and DXF are written on the same machine.

The same model gives the same sheet on every run.

Run it again on an unchanged assembly and the drawings come back identical, so a sheet can be checked once and relied on.
