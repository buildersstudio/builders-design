The drawgen record

One record per part, across every system you run.

What you paid is in your ERP. What you quoted is in your CRM. What the part actually is sits in the CAD model. The drawgen record joins them, so a price you paid last year attaches to the same part in the job you are quoting now. Nobody types anything in.

Talk to us

What it is

One record per part, joined on the part's shape rather than its number, so it works even when part numbers are a mess.

Two ways in

The add-in

inside SOLIDWORKS, used by the engineer. Produces the BOM and the drawings, and quietly writes the part record.

The platform

in the browser, used by the MD, sales and purchasing. Holds every job side by side, joined to purchase history.

The drawgen record

What crosses

The measurements, not the models. Your CAD files stay on your machine, and the whole thing can run on your own server.
