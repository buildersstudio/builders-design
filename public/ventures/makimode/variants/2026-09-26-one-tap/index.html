What goes quiet

Block everything, or choose your own.

You pick once in the app. After that, the tap does the rest on every device signed in to your account.

iPhoneApps are blocked through Screen Time, even when your phone is locked and the app is closed.
MacThe Mac app runs your device, and the free browser helper blocks sites in Chrome or Edge.
WindowsThe MakiMode extension for Chrome or Edge connects to the device and blocks your chosen sites.
FeedsThe extension can also hide addictive feeds, like YouTube and Instagram.

Focusing in two minutes.

Pick your device and follow three short steps. The Mac and iPhone apps are free, and there is no subscription.

Mac

macOS 15 or later
  1. Download the Mac app and drag it into Applications.
  2. Plug in your MakiMode with the USB-C cable and sign in.
  3. Add the browser helper for Chrome or Edge, then tap to focus.

iPhone

iOS 17 or later
  1. Get the app on the App Store and sign in with your Mac account.
  2. Allow Screen Time, so MakiMode can block apps.
  3. Choose what to block, then tap your device to focus.

Windows

Chrome or Edge
  1. Add the MakiMode extension and sign in.
  2. Plug in your MakiMode over USB-C and click Connect.
  3. Tap your device. Your chosen sites are blocked, in sync with the display.
