Five ways out. None of them work.

Most focus tools are easy to ignore. MakiMode blocks on every screen you own at once, and it keeps a face on your desk that reacts to you.

Way out 01
"I will just check Instagram on my phone."

The maki is not impressed. Your phone was blocked the moment you tapped.

What happens

Distracting apps are blocked.

The iPhone app blocks the apps you chose, or everything, through Screen Time. It works even when the phone is locked and the app is closed.

Way out 02
"Fine. I will open it on my laptop."

One eyebrow goes up. The same tap reached your Mac, in sync.

What happens

The same sites are blocked on your computer.

On a Mac, the free browser helper blocks distracting websites in Chrome or Edge. On Windows, the MakiMode extension connects to the device and does the same.

Way out 03
"YouTube is for work. One video."

A long, slow squint. You can watch the one video. The rabbit hole next to it is gone.

What happens

The feed disappears, not the site.

The MakiMode browser extension can hide addictive feeds, like YouTube and Instagram, so you stay in the zone without losing the page you actually need.

Way out 04
"I will just switch focus off."

You can. Tap again when you are done. Leaving early just takes a little more than a tap.

What happens

You have to hold to exit focus.

In the iPhone app, ending a session early is a press and hold, not a quick tap, so it is hard to cheat your way out on impulse. Try it.

Press and keep holding.
