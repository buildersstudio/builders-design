A little sushi roll for your desk: tap it, and your most distracting apps and sites go quiet on your Mac, your iPhone and your browser.

## Product

MakiMode is a small desk device shaped like a maki roll, with a round display in the middle that shows a kawaii face which reacts to you and shows others that you are in focus. One tap on the device starts a focus session, and the free Mac and iPhone apps block the distracting apps and websites you chose, in sync, through Screen Time on the iPhone and a browser helper in Chrome or Edge; on Windows a Chrome extension does the same. The display connects over USB-C or Bluetooth, there is no subscription, and on the iPhone you have to hold to exit focus, so it is hard to cheat your way out. The box holds the device, a USB-C cable, a quick-start guide and a set of stickers. It launches on Kickstarter in September 2026 at a €79 founder price (€120 at retail), with the first batch shipping in November 2026, and it is made in the Netherlands.

## Customer

People who work at a Mac or PC with an iPhone next to them and lose their focus to the apps and sites they open without thinking, and who want a focus tool that does not live on their phone.
