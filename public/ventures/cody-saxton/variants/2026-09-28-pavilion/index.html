    Thoughts

    Notes from the studio — fragments on everything: art, philosophy, process to influences and relevant tangents. Posts will appear here as they're written.

    10 September 2026

    “Let everything happen to you: beauty, terror. Just keep going. No feeling is final.” — Rainer Maria Rilke (exerpt from Go To The Limits of Your Longing)

    28 August 2026

    With every trip around the sun, I think of all the good to come. Planting trees and helping friends, building Gaia up again.

    15 August 2026

    Leaping from a bridge into the Aare River in Bern, Switzerland was transformational...it feels like a part of me from long ago woke up from hibernation. I hesitated, nervous, and then remembered: "You don't have to go alone." When two women stepped over the railing, I broke with my habit of hyperindependence and asked to jump with them. They said "Of Course!"" And off we went, into the beautiful, clear, racing water. The power of secure attachment...can't say how much I feel glad for all I have learned over these years.

    15 July 2026

    Overnight train to Zurich, onto Milan...then trekking in the Italian Alps with silly friends. The mountains hold some power that exists nowhere in The Netherlands---they charge my battery with something so necessary. The lowlands have convenient bicycling and interesting international culture, among other things...but the nature ain't nature-ing. The word soul, or maybe the sense of connectedness to our Earth, tends to feel watery and diffuse in the swamp.

    22 June 2026

    Art is to make and speak and act in alignment with your highest-held values—it is real freedom.

    21 May 2026

    “Everything is art. Everything is politics.” — Ai Weiwei

    19 May 2026

    Ideas should be shared, explored and examined with others. I see each of us as an entirely unique little sensor of the specifics our particular nook of the universe--the view from "in here". When we transmit our condition and collected data--our perspective--the collective can include it to gain an unbiased understanding of the world. When we are silent, our view of the universe is wasted.
