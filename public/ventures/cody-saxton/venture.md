Cody Saxton, Illuminator Omnimedia: art and illustrations to illuminate ways toward a healed world, based in Amsterdam.

## Product
Original works on paper, sold unframed through the site with payment by Mollie or IBAN: ink-lings (brush and sumi ink on rice paper, 2024 onward), color wonderings (watercolor and gouache, 2023 onward) and terracotta subconscious (earth pigments and oil pastel, 2025 onward). 10% of sales go to reforestation and degrowth efforts. The site also carries small in-person workshops on meaning making and creativity, studio notes (thoughts), news, and exhibitions and press.

## Customer
Collectors and people who follow the practice, in Amsterdam and online, plus venues and participants for workshops. Live contact through Instagram @codysaxtonart or invigorated@proton.me.
