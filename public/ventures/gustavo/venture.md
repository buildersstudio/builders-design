Working name. Security and compliance that keep pace with teams shipping AI-written code, in discovery.

## Product

The concept is an automated security layer across the software development lifecycle that combines AI with deterministic checks to find and fix vulnerabilities in what a team ships. It is meant to run without blocking the release, to show what each release touched and where the security risks sit, and to turn findings into evidence for frameworks such as GDPR, SOC 2 and ISO 27001. The venture is in problem discovery: no product has been shipped, priced or piloted yet, and the scope is still being chosen between code, the delivery pipeline and running infrastructure.

## Customer

Small software teams that ship several times a week with coding agents and meet security and compliance demands from customers, partners or regulators, from early-stage startups selling to enterprises to vendors serving regulated sectors such as banking.
