The AI venture studio in Rotterdam that co-founds enterprise AI companies with bold founders.

## Product

Builders is an AI venture studio that starts companies with founders and helps early ventures scale. It works across three pillars: the Studio, which co-founds and builds the companies; the Network, a community of founders, operators and investors with dedicated CTO and Navigators groups; and Capital, which invests at seed stage alongside the studio. Every venture runs on VSI, the studio's own operating layer, which tracks strategic positions, scores the evidence from customer conversations and gives founders decision-grade intelligence.

## Customer

Founders who want to build an enterprise AI company with a co-founding team behind them, and the investors and operators who join those companies through the network.
