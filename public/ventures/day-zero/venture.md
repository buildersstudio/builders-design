The monthly build night of Builders, where founders and young technical talent come together to build AI ventures.

## Product

Day Zero is an evening at Builders in Rotterdam, held once a month, where founders and technical builders sit down together and build AI ventures through the night. It is a derivation of the Builders studio itself: the same room, the same people and the same bias for shipping, compressed into one night.

## Customer

Founders with an idea or an early product, and technical youngsters who want to build with them: engineers, designers and makers early in their careers who would rather ship something than talk about it.
