What happens on the night

The studio,
in one night.

Once a month, founders and technical builders sit down together at Builders in Rotterdam and build AI ventures through the night. The same room, the same people and the same bias for shipping as the studio itself.

01

Bring an idea, or the skills to build one

Founders arrive with an idea or an early product. Engineers, designers and makers arrive ready to build it with them.

02

Sit down side by side

At the long tables, the kitchen counter and the whiteboards of the Builders office, a founder and a builder work on the same thing.

03

Build past midnight

Build nights at Builders run past midnight. The point is to leave with something built rather than something discussed.

Who it is for

Two kinds of people,

For founders

You have the idea

Founders with an idea or an early product, who want technical people in the room to build it with.

For technical youngsters

You have the hands

Engineers, designers and makers early in their careers who would rather ship something than talk about it.
