README.md

Bring an idea or bring the skills. Build the venture tonight.

What

A build night at Builders, once a month. The same room, the same people and the same bias for shipping as the studio, compressed into one night.

Who

Founders with an idea or an early product, and engineers, designers and makers early in their careers who would rather ship than talk.

From edition 03

Every night gets a theme and a guest who speaks for that topic or industry. The theme decides what gets built.
