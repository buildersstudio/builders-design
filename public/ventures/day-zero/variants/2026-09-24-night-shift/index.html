The night

From doors open
to demos.

One evening a month, the studio stays open late. What happens in it follows the same shape every time.

00Evening

Doors open

The Builders office opens for the night. Founders and builders arrive, find a seat and get a sense of who is in the room.

01Evening

Teams form

Founders bring an idea or an early product. Engineers, designers and makers choose what they want to build and sit down with them.

02Through the night

Build

Heads down, in the same room, with the same bias for shipping that runs the studio every other day of the month.

03End of the night

Demos

What was built is shown to the room, working, as it stands when the night is over.

The room

The Builders office,
after hours.

Day Zero happens at Builders HQ in Rotterdam, beside Central Station: the same room, the same people and the same bias for shipping, compressed into one night.

Builders HQ · Rotterdam

The rest of the month, this is where the studio co-founds its AI ventures from day zero. For one night, the desks open to the people who come to build.
