01 · The night

The studio,
in one night.

Day Zero is a derivation of the Builders studio itself: the same room, the same people and the same bias for shipping, held once a month.

01

Founders and builders sit down together.

Founders come with an idea or an early product. Technical builders come ready to build it with them.

02

AI ventures get built through the night.

Teams work side by side at Builders in Rotterdam, in the same room where the studio builds its companies every day.

03

Something ships.

The night carries the studio's bias for shipping, so it is spent building the thing rather than talking about it.
