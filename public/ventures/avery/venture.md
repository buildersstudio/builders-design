## Product

Avery is an AI-native hiring intelligence platform for Europe that finds, evaluates and reaches candidates for open roles. Recruiters describe the role in plain language instead of writing boolean strings, and search a database of more than 125 million European professionals. Each match is scored across seven dimensions, with written reasons a recruiter can share with the hiring manager. A Talent Hub keeps every candidate the team has worked with searchable and current, and re-matches them when a new role fits, while outreach runs as personal messages with human checks wherever the team wants them. The platform syncs in both directions with more than 50 ATS and recruiting tools.

## Customer

Recruitment agencies, freelance recruiters, in-house hiring teams and consultancy teams that run their own recruitment, with a focus on hiring in Europe.
