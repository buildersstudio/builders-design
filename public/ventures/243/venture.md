A short, generous read of your personality: sixteen everyday scenes in about three minutes, and in return a shape that is yours, one of 243 names, and a sixteen-page read of who you are and how you tend to be in love, friendship, family, work and money.

## Product

243 is a web app, built by the studio, that asks sixteen quick scenes such as "Someone you are close to has gone quiet on you, and you do not know why", each with three or four answers, and asks you to answer for your usual self. The answers place you on five dimensions with three positions each: Move (toward, against, away) and Eye (guard, level, reach) set one of nine cores, from The Keeper to The Explorer, and three sharpeners, Compass, Frame and Volume, give the core one of 27 adjectives, so every result is one of 243 named shapes such as The Steadfast Keeper, drawn as a coloured shape whose five points sit on three rings. The result is a sixteen-page read in four parts: who you are (your read, a portrait, your shape, the engine underneath and your strengths), your life (love, friends, family, work, money, and body, rest and energy), growing from here (how other people perceive you, when your usual move tips too far, and what helps), and a final page to share the result by link and give feedback on how well it read you. The name is optional, each finished result is saved on the device and sent with any feedback to a collector the studio can review, the nine cores can be browsed on their own, and the read is written with the help of AI and presented as a reflective tool rather than a clinical assessment.

## Customer

People curious about themselves and the people close to them who want a read that is quick to take and specific enough to recognise, and who will share it with a partner, friend or colleague to see whether it is right.
