Two questions decide your core. How you move, and what you notice first.

Columns · the 27 adjectives

Three sharpeners give your core its word.

Compass is whose judgment you trust. Frame is how you hold plans. Volume is how loudly feelings register. Each combination is one word, shared across the nine cores, so a Keeper can be Steadfast, Tender or Untamed.
