01Part 1 · Who you are

A portrait you would recognise, and a few things you may not have connected.

Portrait
You are the calm in most rooms you enter, and you produce it without effort or announcement. You see the reasonable version of almost everyone, including people who are at odds with each other. The cost is subtle and slow: your own position tends to stay unspoken.

03Part 3 · Growing from here

How it feels from the inside, and how it lands on the people close to you.

How you see itHow it lands on others
"I am flexible."
"I cannot locate their actual preference, so every decision lands on me."
"I am keeping the peace."
"Nothing ever gets decided, and I am starting to feel like the only one who cares."
"I am being fair."
"Sometimes I do not want fair. I want them on my side."
When someone asks what you wantAnswer before you have checked what they want.

The friction comes later, when nobody can find your preference.

When you have agreed to something you will not doSay the no now, while it is still cheap.

A late no costs the other person a plan. An early one costs them a sentence.

When a decision has waited more than a monthMake one small irreversible move.

Book it, send it, sign it. Comfort does not generate deadlines on its own.
