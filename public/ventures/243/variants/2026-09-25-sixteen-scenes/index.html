How it reads you

Five dimensions, three positions each. That is your shape.

Each point of the shape is one dimension, and it sits on the inner, middle or outer ring depending on where you landed. Move and Eye place you in one of nine cores. Compass, Frame and Volume give your core its adjective.

9 cores × 27 adjectives = 243 shapes, and every one of them has its own name, from The Unshakable Keeper to The Radiant Explorer.
What you get back

A sixteen-page read, in four parts.

Part 1

Who you are

Your read, a portrait, your shape explained point by point, the engine running underneath and your strengths.

Part 2

Your life

The same shape in six rooms: love, friends, family, work, money, and body, rest and energy.

Part 3

Growing from here

How other people perceive you, what happens when your usual move is taken too far, and small adjustments that tend to help.

Part 4

Before you go

A link to share your result, and a few questions on how well it read you.
